*Best fit: JLR dossier Layer 3/4, or value pyramid Level 4 if JLR gets its own deck.*

## How to use this file in the skills

- **account-dossier**: when Layer 3 research (search 12) finds no account-specific
  copilot/AI rollout numbers, cite the nearest peer from this file instead of leaving
  the section thin — label it clearly as peer evidence, not the account's own.
- **value-pyramid**: Level 4 needs exactly 6 stats. If fewer than 6 account-specific
  proof points surface, fill remaining slots from this file, visually distinguished
  (e.g. "industry benchmark" tag) from the account's own disclosed numbers — never
  blend the two without labelling which is which.
- Numbers here are dated at point of writing (Sept 2026) and mostly reflect 2025–2026
  disclosures. Re-verify anything older than 90 days before it goes in a live deck,
  per the skills' own RAG health rules.
